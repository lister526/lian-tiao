# lian-tiao (链条.skill)

**Systematic, rational argumentation framework distilled from X user [@Jueshengshenlan](https://x.com/Jueshengshenlan) (链条)**

A highly usable skill for AI agents (Grok, Claude, local models) and human debaters who need calm, evidence-based, historically grounded responses when defending Chinese positions against common hostile narratives.

> Logic first · Long civilizational horizon · Internal agency over external salvation

## Why this exists

Most online China-related arguments degenerate into slogans or emotional nationalism. This project extracts the actual high-performing patterns used by one of the most effective Chinese accounts on X (high engagement, calm tone, strong logical structure) and turns them into a reusable system.

## Quick Start

### For AI Agents
Just load `SKILL.md`. It is written in the standard skill format.

### For Humans
1. Read the **Core Principles** in `SKILL.md`
2. Use `references/prompt-templates.md` for ready-made reply structures by country / attack type
3. Reference `references/core-examples.md` and `more-examples.md` for real high-engagement examples

## Core Principles (Summary)

1. **Reality over Narrative** — The world runs on physics, not values.
2. **Internal Agency** — An egg broken from the outside is food; from the inside is life.
3. **Civilization-state** — Continuous civilization, not Westphalian nation-state.
4. **Reverse Indicator** — Chronic China-collapse prophets are reliable reverse signals.
5. **Selective Engagement** — Defeat the right opponent. Do not fight everyone.
6. **Global South Solidarity** — Stand with the Global South against sea-power extraction.

## What it is good at

- Democracy superiority lectures
- Taiwan / Tibet / Xinjiang sovereignty attacks
- Industrial & military capability skepticism
- Western moral lectures and "rules-based order" framing
- Attempts to drive wedges between China and Global South countries
- Handling professional China-bashers (Gordon Chang style)

## Tone

Prefer calm, slightly formal openings ("亲爱的XX朋友"), historical storytelling, hard data, precise humor, and ending with goodwill toward ordinary people of the other country while rejecting elite narratives.

## Origin

All core metaphors (egg, oak trees, reverse-indicator), tone, and framing are distilled from publicly available posts by @Jueshengshenlan (2024–2026). This repository is a systematic reorganization for practical reuse.
