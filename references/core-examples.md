# Core High-Engagement Examples from 链条

## 1. Highest-impact reply (Brazil internal agency)
> 谢谢巴西人信任中国，但我想告诉你们:
> 我们的干预是没有用的，就好像一个蛋，从外面破开是食物，从里面破开，才是生命。
> 蜕变，新生，只能从内部发生。
> 不要把希望寄托在外部，寄托在选票上。
> 当你们跨过选票，相信巴西真正的主宰，是你们的力量与勇气时，一切都将改变。
> 加油💪🇨🇳❤️🇧🇷

Key technique: Egg metaphor + rejection of external salvation + affirmation of local agency.

## 2. Gordon Chang reverse-indicator
> 此君在2001年8月写了一本《中国即将崩溃》... 他就是一个完美的反向指标。
> 所以，当他说出这个预言，博索纳罗家族就永远不可能崛起了😂

Key technique: Turn the opponent’s preferred authority into a reliable negative signal.

## 3. Democracy structural critique
Long-form reply explaining how multi-party competition prioritizes blocking over solving, leading to polarization and eventual strongman demand. Ends with the bread-price threshold for social radicalization.

## 4. Tibet / France double standard
> 你要的是1%的，达赖这种奴隶主的自由，还是99%西藏人民的自由？
> ... 法国才是侵略者 (Sankara case with concrete post-assassination outcomes).

Key technique: Flip the moral accusation with a specific, documented historical parallel from the accuser’s own history.

## 5. Sea power / Heartland framing
Explain why sea powers systematically seek to fragment Eurasia (identity politics as soft tool, NATO expansion, etc.). Position Chinese-Russian coordination as structural response, not sentiment.

## 6. National character observations
Calm, observational summaries of interaction patterns with different countries’ netizens (Turkey, Germany/France, Japan, USA, India, Russia, Brazil, China). Used to deflate stereotypes without descending into pure insult.
