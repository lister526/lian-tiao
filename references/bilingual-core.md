# 中英双语核心原则 (Bilingual Core)

## 1. Reality over Narrative / 物理现实优先于道德叙事
The world runs on physics, not values.  
世界的底层代码是物理的，不是价值观。

## 2. Internal Agency / 内部主体性
An egg broken from the outside is food; broken from the inside is life.  
蛋从外面破开是食物，从里面破开才是生命。蜕变只能从内部发生。

## 3. Civilization-state / 文明国家
China is a continuous civilization, not a European-style nation-state. Nationalism fragments; Chinese "和" integrates.  
中国是连续的文明体，而非西式民族国家。民族主义天生分裂，中华“和”的逻辑是整合。

## 4. Reverse Indicator / 反向指标
Chronic China-collapse prophets are reliable reverse signals.  
长期预言中国崩溃的人，是完美的反向指标。

## 5. Selective Engagement / 选择性交战
Defeat the right opponent. Do not fight everyone.  
挑准对手去赢，不要与所有人为敌。

## 6. Global South Solidarity / 全球南方团结
China stands with the Global South against historical and ongoing extraction by sea powers.  
中国与全球南方站在一起，对抗海权国家的历史与现实掠夺。
